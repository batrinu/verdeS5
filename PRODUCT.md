# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Citizens of Sector 5, Bucharest** — on their phones, outdoors or at home, adopting and caring for street trees. Their job: find a tree that needs help, log a watering with photo proof, feel their care counts (points, guardian levels, rewards).
- **City-hall / council staff** — the pitch audience and the "Mod Consiliu" persona: evaluating whether the platform is credible municipal infrastructure. Their job: see coverage, activity, and accountability at a glance.
- **Corporate sponsor prospects** — shown the ESG dashboard: verifiable survival evidence (photo-proofed care logs, CO₂ estimates, CSV export) rather than planting certificates.

## Product Purpose

A civic tree-care platform positioned as *"Sector 3 plants; Sector 5 keeps trees alive."*
Currently a deployed pitch demo for Sector 5 city hall. Success = the demo reads as a
real, credible product: citizens would use it daily, the council trusts the data, and
sponsors see auditable ESG value. Local-first: every surface must work fully offline
from seed data.

## Brand Personality

**Warm civic-tech**: trustworthy, warm, alive. Institutional credibility for the
council; human and hopeful for citizens — trees have voices, care is celebrated,
neighborhoods compete in good faith. Romanian voice throughout, plain-language and
friendly, never bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

- **Generic government portals** — gray bureaucratic forms-and-tables municipal sites
  (primarie.ro energy). This demo exists to contrast with them.
- **Corporate ESG greenwash** — stock leaf imagery, vague green gradients,
  sustainability-report aesthetics with no real data underneath.
- **Childish gamification** — mascots, confetti, cartoon badges. Playfulness must never
  undermine credibility with the council.

## Design Principles

1. **The tree is the hero** — every surface centers living tree profiles (water status,
   care history, impact), not abstract stats. Data serves the tree's story.
2. **Evidence over decoration** — the differentiator is proof (photo logs, survival
   rates, honest offline states). Never fake success; label estimates „estimat".
3. **Two audiences, one surface** — citizen warmth and council gravitas coexist; the
   persona split (Cetățean/Consiliu) is a lens, not two products.
4. **Quiet gamification** — points, levels and challenges motivate without shouting;
   recognition and real privileges over badges and confetti.
5. **Works in the field** — one hand, bright sun, weak signal: mobile-first touch
   targets, local-first data, the demo never breaks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA (EU public-sector requirement — also a pitch selling point). Existing
practices to preserve: `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives, modal focus traps with
Escape, ≥44px touch targets, descriptive map-marker labels, `role`/`aria` on
interactive widgets. Color is never the only signal (water-status legend pairs color
with text labels).
